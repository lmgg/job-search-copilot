# Product Manager, Growth
**Company:** Fitloop (fictional) · **Location:** Manchester, hybrid · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role
Fitloop is a consumer fitness app with 2 million monthly users. You will lead activation and retention, working with a cross-functional growth team.

## What you will do
- Own activation and week-4 retention metrics.
- Run a high tempo of experiments across onboarding, notifications and paywalls.
- Partner with marketing and data science on lifecycle campaigns.
- Share results and learnings with the wider company.

## What we are looking for
- 3+ years in product, with a track record of growth experiments.
- Strong analytical skills; SQL required.
- Experience with consumer mobile apps (iOS/Android).
- Nice to have: subscription or freemium monetisation experience.

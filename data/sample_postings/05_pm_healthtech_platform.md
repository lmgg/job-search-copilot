# Product Manager, Clinician Tools
**Company:** CareBridge (fictional) · **Location:** Remote (UK) · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role
CareBridge builds scheduling and patient-record tools for NHS-partner clinics. You will improve the daily workflow of clinicians and admin staff.

## What you will do
- Shadow clinicians and admin staff to map workflows and pain points.
- Prioritise a roadmap that balances usability, safety and compliance.
- Work with clinical safety officers on risk assessments (DCB0129).
- Ship improvements with a squad of 5 engineers and a designer.

## What we are looking for
- 3+ years as a product manager.
- Experience in healthcare or another regulated industry.
- Strong user research skills with non-technical users.
- Nice to have: knowledge of NHS systems or interoperability standards (FHIR).

# Senior Product Manager, AI Platform
**Company:** Lumen Labs (fictional) · **Location:** Remote (UK/EU) · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role
We are building internal tooling that lets product teams ship LLM-powered features safely. You will define how teams evaluate, monitor and deploy models.

## What you will do
- Own the roadmap for our evaluation and prompt-management platform.
- Interview internal engineering teams to understand their workflows.
- Define quality metrics for AI features with ML engineers.
- Write clear specs and make trade-offs between speed, cost and safety.

## What we are looking for
- 6+ years in product, including 2+ on technical or platform products.
- Hands-on familiarity with LLMs, evaluation methods and prompt design.
- Able to read Python and talk API design with engineers.
- Nice to have: experience with MLOps or developer tools.

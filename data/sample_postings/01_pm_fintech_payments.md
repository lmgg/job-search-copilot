# Product Manager, Payments
**Company:** Coinwell (fictional) · **Location:** London, hybrid · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role
You will own our checkout and payments experience for 50,000 small merchants. You will work with an engineering squad of 7 and a designer to reduce payment failures and improve conversion.

## What you will do
- Own the payments roadmap and set quarterly goals.
- Run discovery with merchants and turn insights into clear requirements.
- Design and analyse experiments on checkout flows.
- Work with risk and compliance on PSD2 and fraud controls.

## What we are looking for
- 4+ years as a product manager, ideally in fintech or payments.
- Comfortable with SQL and product analytics tools.
- Experience working with regulated teams (compliance, risk, legal).
- Nice to have: knowledge of card networks or open banking.

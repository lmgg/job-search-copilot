# Senior Product Manager, Data & Reporting
**Company:** Stackfield (fictional) · **Location:** London, hybrid · **Type:** Full-time

## About the role
Our customers rely on Stackfield reports to run their operations. You will own the reporting and data-export experience for 3,000 business customers.

## What you will do
- Own the vision and roadmap for dashboards, exports and scheduled reports.
- Work with customers and customer success to find the biggest pain points.
- Partner with data engineering on performance and data quality.
- Define success metrics and report on adoption.

## What we are looking for
- 5+ years of B2B SaaS product management.
- Experience shipping analytics or reporting products.
- Comfortable with SQL and data modelling basics.
- Strong stakeholder management across sales, success and engineering.
